Our Story

A heritage of flavor, served with intention.

Thai Corner Restaurant & Bakery has been established in year 2022 by the Seedee’ s family from Thailand. Their passion is to sharing their delicious cooking from a wide range of savory Thai cuisine as well as Thai & international bake goods which would offer Edmontonian unique Menu items created with passion from the chefs.

The authenticity with high quality ingredienes added with passion in cooking from this culinary savvy family would definitely delivery such a uniquely new wonderful flavours to the culinary scene in Edmonton AB.

— Chef Anong Pranee Founder & Executive Chef
